✨  Good news

Uplifting local headlines

REI Co-op opens first Central Coast store in San Luis Obispo

SGB Media

REI's new 22,000-square-foot store at SLO Promenade opened Oct. 25 — the co-op's 29th California store and its first on the Central Coast — with $20,000 donated to The Land Conservancy of SLO and Central Coast Concerned Mountain Bikers to celebrate.

Waterman Village affordable housing project in SLO cleared to proceed

City of San Luis Obispo

After a two-year lawsuit delay, a Sept. 29 court ruling cleared the way for Waterman Village: 20 much-needed low- and very-low-income homes plus restoration of the historic Rosa Butrón Adobe as a community gathering space in downtown SLO.

Four new eateries welcomed the Cal Poly community this summer

Mustang News

Four new spots welcomed the Cal Poly community this fall: Park 1039 reopened on Chorro Street, High St. Headquarters opened a third location, Frankie's Garden Street Caffè debuted downtown, and Little Spoon Creamery scooped its first cones at SLO Public Market.

County finishes new Probation Department HQ in San Luis Obispo

Estero Bay News

The County's new 34,000-square-foot, $40 million Probation Department headquarters on Johnson Avenue finished on schedule and within budget, with upgraded technology, training space and accessibility.
