Tiny Café opens new brick-and-mortar location on Taft Street

Mustang News

Founded by Emily Quady and Colin Brown as a pop-up and trailer, the popular matcha-and-coffee spot has opened its first brick-and-mortar at 1340 Taft St. — open daily 7 AM to 4 PM, with evening gatherings and live music planned.

Mee Heng Low Social Club opens in historic Chinatown

Up + Adam with Adam Montiel

The beloved Palm Street landmark — the heart of SLO's historic Chinatown for nearly a century — reopened Saturday as a social club with mahjong, wine, shopping and pop-up dinners, run by Karson Butler Hospitality Co. with the Gin family.

Downtown SLO is ready to celebrate 50 years of holiday magic

SLO Chamber of Commerce

The $5.8 million Mission Plaza Enhancement Project is complete — new paving, lighting, restrooms and a dining area — just in time for the holiday season, which also marks 50 years of the Downtown SLO Holiday Parade.

Michelin-starred chef brings a new culinary destination to the Central Coast

Visit California

Edna, a new wine-country gathering spot in Edna Valley with a Michelin-starred chef at the helm, opened its restaurant, market, tasting room and distillery in early November — a joint tasting room pouring Dunites, Greengate, Stephen Ross and Vestre wines.
