🐋  Whale watch

Are they seeing whales out there?

Humpbacks are still showing off the Central Coast: Avila Beach Whale Watching found whales on three straight trips Nov. 8–11, capped by a Nov. 11 humpback that swam right under the boat and sang loudly enough to hear above the surface, and Morro Bay's Lost Isle found four humpbacks Nov. 12. But the season's first big winter storm brought 20–25 ft swells Nov. 13–14, pausing trips — Lost Isle's next possible departure is Nov. 21, conditions permitting. Read more →
